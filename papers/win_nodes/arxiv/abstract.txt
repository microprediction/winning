Order-statistic queries and evidence, such as which of several correlated variables is largest or who won a contest, have long resisted exact treatment in Bayes nets. For correlated Gaussian variables the probability that one is the maximum is an orthant probability, which authors in Bayesian optimization and reinforcement learning call intractable and approximate, and rating systems approximate their posteriors by expectation propagation. We show that for structured Gaussian Bayes nets these queries can be computed deterministically in time linear in the number of variables. When the dependence runs through a few shared factors, a recent algorithm for correlated contests gives the probabilities, their derivatives and the likelihood of an observed winner. When the variables form a Markov chain, restricted forward and backward passes give the same probabilities, and on banded precision matrices the law of the maximum and first-passage times. Under a posterior, the same probabilities are the action probabilities of Thompson sampling.
