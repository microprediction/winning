# Win Nodes for Structured Gaussian Bayes Nets Admitting Deterministic Order-Statistic Queries

The n-ary win node W = argmax_i X_i on Gaussian Bayes nets: forward
query, Jacobian and observed-winner likelihood for factor-structured
parents; argmax, law of the maximum and first passage on chains and
bands. Merged in 2026-11 with papers/exact_pom, whose posterior
closures (common random numbers, factor bandit) and stopping and
bandit experiments are now Sections 4 and 7 here.

Numbers trace to committed scripts: research/tridiagonal (chain_argmax.py,
exp1-exp4 numerical checks, exp6_career_peak tennis example and figure)
and research/rs_crn (exp1_stopping, exp2_bandit, exp3_quadrature).
arXiv kit: arxiv/.
