Watch it converge

Pick a covariance ensemble and a field size. The lattice race prices every contestant in a few milliseconds; frequency simulation crawls down its 1/√M slope toward the same answer; and ignoring the correlation never arrives at all. Everything runs in your browser on the same parity-locked engine the Python, R and Rust packages share.

Vertical axis: total-variation distance to the lattice answer (log scale). Horizontal axis: Monte-Carlo draws (log scale). The dashed guide is the 1/√M law. On the dense ensembles the simulation floors at the small grammar-fit residual instead of zero: that visible gap is the honest boundary of the fitted approximation (largest for the AR(1) chain, whose locality structure is the one family the grammar serves worst).

The lattice number quoted below is the full all-$n$ computation, including (for the dense rows) the in-browser eigenfit of the covariance to the race grammar. The simulator draws correlated fields through a Cholesky factor as fast as your machine allows.
