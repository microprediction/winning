Linear normalization against Gaussian renormalization

Every self-contained mathematical claim in the paper, checked in the browser from nothing. No data files, no network, no libraries. The normal law is Hart's algorithm, the contest integral is Simpson's rule on a fine grid, and the inverse map is Newton on the log-share residual. Numbers below are computed when the page loads, so a failing claim shows as a failure.

What is not checked here

The empirical figures, being every held-out log loss in the paper, are not reproducible from a static page: they need the committed data. They are checked instead by demo/check_tables.py, which traces each quoted figure to the run that produced it, and reports any figure printed in a table that no committed run accounts for.
