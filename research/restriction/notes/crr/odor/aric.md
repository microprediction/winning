# ARIC — one variable, NCS14B "Smell test score"

## Citation
Atherosclerosis Risk in Communities (ARIC) Study, NHLBI BioLINCC data dictionary (ARIC_2026a).
Not a publication.

## Domain and stimuli
Olfaction. 12-item Sniffin' Sticks screening administered at a single visit.

## Master response set and restricted response sets (nested, overlapping, or a relabelling)
Fixed 4-alternative list per item, but not recoverable — no per-item variables are released.

## What numbers are printed or deposited (which tables/files, counts or proportions, per subject or pooled)
**One score variable, `NCS14B`.** The complete 11.8 MB dictionary was grepped: the only smell-related
fields are NCS14, NCS14A, NCS14B, NCS14C/C1, NCS14D, NCS14E/E1, NCS14F/F1/F2 — administration flags and
a total. **No per-item anything.**

## Access (a DIRECT url you fetched; open, paywalled, or Wayback-only)

11.8 MB, grepped. Study page: https://biolincc.nhlbi.nih.gov/studies/aric/ Full data via
aricdata@unc.edu (access-controlled).

## Usability verdict (usable now / needs digitizing / needs library access / unusable, and why)
**Unusable.** Summary score only. Kamath's 12-item norms work using ARIC is likewise access-controlled
and reports only totals.
