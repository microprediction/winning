# Rush ROS/MAP (RADC) — one variable, total_smell_test, range 0–12

## Citation
Rush Alzheimer's Disease Center, Religious Orders Study and Rush Memory and Aging Project (ROS/MAP).
RADC variable documentation. Not a publication.

## Domain and stimuli
Olfaction. Brief Smell Identification Test (B-SIT), 12 items, 4-AFC.

## Master response set and restricted response sets (nested, overlapping, or a relabelling)
Fixed 4-alternative list per item. Irrelevant in practice, because the chosen descriptor is not
retained — see below.

## What numbers are printed or deposited (which tables/files, counts or proportions, per subject or pooled)
**A single variable, `total_smell_test`, range 0–12.** No per-item variables exist. There is no
olfaction form dictionary at RADC at all. The structural cause is the B-SIT data model itself, which
codes items 0=Incorrect/1=Correct and discards the chosen descriptor — see `nda_sniffin01.md`.

## Usability verdict (usable now / needs digitizing / needs library access / unusable, and why)
**Unusable.** Even with full data access the granularity is a 0–12 sum. Nothing to digitize, nothing to
request.
