# Sniffin' Sticks normative studies (Hummel 2007; Oleszkiewicz 2019) — norms tables only

## Citation
Hummel T, Kobal G, Gudziol H, Mackay-Sim A. "Normative data for the 'Sniffin' Sticks' including tests of
odor identification, odor discrimination, and olfactory thresholds: an upgrade based on a group of more
than 3,000 subjects." *European Archives of Oto-Rhino-Laryngology* 2007;264(3):237–243. PMID 17021776.
Oleszkiewicz A, Schriever VA, Croy I, Hähner A, Hummel T. "Updated Sniffin' Sticks normative data based
on an extended sample of 9139 subjects." *Eur Arch Otorhinolaryngol* 2019;276(3):719–728. PMID 30554358.
Also checked: Kamath et al. 2024 normative data for the 12-item test in older adults (*Arch Clin
Neuropsychol* 39(3):335), which uses access-controlled ARIC data.

## Domain and stimuli
Olfaction. Sniffin' Sticks battery, 16-item identification subtest at 4-AFC, in very large normative
samples (3,000+ and 9,139 subjects respectively).

## Master response set and restricted response sets (nested, overlapping, or a relabelling)
**Fixed 4 alternatives.** These papers define the reference 4-AFC descriptor lists that
`negoias_2010.md` built its 3-AFC and 6-AFC conditions from, so they are the canonical source for the
**M(4)** menu — but they contain no manipulation.

## What numbers are printed or deposited (which tables/files, counts or proportions, per subject or pooled)
**Percentile norms for total scores, by age band and sex. Pooled, no per-item and no per-descriptor
data, nothing deposited.** Despite n = 9,139 in the later paper, not a single per-descriptor number is
published.

## Access (a DIRECT url you fetched; open, paywalled, or Wayback-only)

## Usability verdict (usable now / needs digitizing / needs library access / unusable, and why)
**Unusable for choice shares.** Norms tables are digitizable but contain only score percentiles, which is
useless for menu-effect work. Recorded so the large sample sizes do not tempt a re-search: n = 9,139 with
zero descriptor-level information.

Use these only to confirm the standard 4-AFC descriptor lists when certifying the `negoias_2010.md`
nesting claim.

## What the authors concluded, quoted verbatim where possible
Both papers conclude that reference to **narrow age groups** increases the diagnostic validity of the
test, and provide updated age- and sex-stratified percentile norms for that purpose.
