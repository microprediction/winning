# Health ABC — 12-item B-SIT, Year 3 only, summary score in all published work

## Citation
Health, Aging and Body Composition (Health ABC) Study. NIA study documentation. Not a publication.

## Domain and stimuli
Olfaction. Brief Smell Identification Test (B-SIT), 12 items, 4-AFC, administered at Year 3 only.

## Master response set and restricted response sets (nested, overlapping, or a relabelling)
Fixed 4-alternative list per item; chosen descriptor almost certainly not retained, since the B-SIT
data model records correct/incorrect only (see `nda_sniffin01.md`).

## What numbers are printed or deposited (which tables/files, counts or proportions, per subject or pooled)
All published Health ABC olfaction work uses the **0–12 total score**. Codebooks are not posted publicly
— they are supplied by email request only, so per-item availability could not be confirmed either way.

## Access (a DIRECT url you fetched; open, paywalled, or Wayback-only)
https://www.nia.nih.gov/healthabc-study — **open** landing page; codebooks by request
(simonsickel@grc.nia.nih.gov). No public variable list.

## Usability verdict (usable now / needs digitizing / needs library access / unusable, and why)
**Effectively unusable.** Would require an email just to learn whether per-item data exists, and the
B-SIT schema makes a negative answer very likely. Low priority; do not pursue unless a B-SIT cohort
becomes essential for some other reason.

## What the authors concluded, quoted verbatim where possible
Not applicable — study documentation.
