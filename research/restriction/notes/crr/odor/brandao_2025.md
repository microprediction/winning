# Brandão et al. 2025 — Brazilian Sniffin' Sticks Rasch data, binary only

## Citation
de Paula Brandão PR, et al. "Sniffin' sticks smell identification test: exploring measurement properties
in an adult Brazilian healthcare professionals sample." *PeerJ* 2025;13:e19733. PMC12577572.
Data deposit: doi:10.5281/zenodo.15384922.

## Domain and stimuli
Olfaction. Sniffin' Sticks identification, Brazilian Portuguese, "four Brazilian Portuguese verbal
descriptor options per odorant". Adult healthcare professionals.

## Master response set and restricted response sets (nested, overlapping, or a relabelling)
**Fixed 4 alternatives per odor.** The Brazilian adaptation substitutes culturally appropriate
descriptor words, so relative to the German original it is a **relabelling** — but only one version was
administered here, so no within-study comparison is possible.

## What numbers are printed or deposited (which tables/files, counts or proportions, per subject or pooled)
**Per-subject but binary.** I downloaded `peerj-104137-raw_data_sniffing_data.csv` (5,047 B) and parsed
it: **143 subjects × 15 items, semicolon-delimited, values 0/1 only** (an `orange` column is absent).
Regression analyses treat responses as "0 = incorrect, 1 = correct", confirming descriptor selections
were not retained in the analyzed file.

## Usability verdict (usable now / needs digitizing / needs library access / unusable, and why)
**Unusable for choice shares** — binary. Small n (143) too. Recorded as definitively checked, by direct
download and parse, so it is not re-examined.

## What the authors concluded, quoted verbatim where possible
A Rasch/measurement-properties study concluding the Brazilian Portuguese identification subtest has
acceptable psychometric properties in this sample, with some items functioning poorly. Distractor choice
is not analyzed.
