## Domain and stimuli

Absolute identification of horizontal lines, 10 stimuli. Two experiments. Sequential
dependencies analysed with multivariate information transmission.

## Master response set and restricted response sets (nested, overlapping, or a relabelling)

**No restriction.** 10 stimuli and 10 responses throughout. The manipulation is the
amount of *stimulus* information — line luminance across six levels in Experiment 1, and
high/low information crossed with feedback/no-feedback plus a guessing condition in
Experiment 2. Response set size is constant.

## What numbers are printed or deposited (which tables/files, counts or proportions, per subject or pooled)

Information-theoretic decompositions, not matrices.

## What the authors concluded, quoted verbatim where possible

On the constant total, which is the paper's actual claim:

> "The magnitude of the dependency of R_n on S_n-1 and R_n-1 was inversely proportional to
> that of information transmission from S_n to R_n... Interestingly, the sum of information
> transmission from S_n, S_n-1, and R_n-1 to R_n was always about 2.5 bits, without respect
> to the variance of each term. This result could have arisen from the operation of a
> limited-capacity response process."
