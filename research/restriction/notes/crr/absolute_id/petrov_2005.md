## Citation

Petrov, A. A., & Anderson, J. R. (2005). The dynamics of scaling: A memory-based anchor
model of category rating and absolute identification. *Psychological Review*, 112(2),
383-416.

## Domain and stimuli

Category rating and absolute identification of dots varying in separation. The ANCHOR
model. Presentation frequency of individual stimuli is manipulated.

## Master response set and restricted response sets (nested, overlapping, or a relabelling)

**No response-set-size manipulation.** The number of response categories is fixed at 7
throughout. The manipulation is presentation frequency, and per Dodds et al. (2011) it "was
counterbalanced over short time scales, such that long-term learning effects... would not be
expected in longer time-scale averages". No master/restricted structure.
