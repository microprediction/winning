Let independent blocks X_B=mu_B+R_B Z_B have explicit rank-two roots
with arbitrary row count b>=2. Condition on scalar observation
U=sum h_B' Z_B + sqrt(1-sum ||h_B||^2) epsilon=0, h_B=R_B' beta_B,
where sum ||h_B||^2<=1. C=blockdiag(R_B R_B'), target C-C beta beta' C.
This is dense and potentially exactly singular; no structural fit.
Former intrinsic code accepted at most three rows per block. A large
rank-two block can be represented by positive local factors and zero
private variance, but the existing conditional-private-density
quadrature does not implement this interval boundary; the global
negative observation is not part of the positive shared-field grammar.

For row i let s_i=||R_i||, e_i=R_i/s_i, q_i=(-e_i[1],e_i[0]).
On X_i=t we have Z=e_i (t-mu_i)/s_i+q_i z. Rival j imposes
v_j z>(1-reg_j)t+reg_j mu_i-mu_j, v_j=R_j q_i,
reg_j=R_j e_i/s_i. Intersect all half-lines into one interval
(l_i(t),u_i(t)). Lower bound is upper envelope of affine lines
for v_j>0; upper bound is negative of upper envelope of negated lines
for v_j<0. Monotone slope stack constructs each envelope O(d log d)
for d constraints, evaluation O(log d) via breakpoints. Split t grid
at active segment switches, lower/upper crossings and normal-scale
knots. Boundary density
  g_i(t,k)=phi((t-mu_i)/s_i)/s_i *
    exp(ik h_B'e_i (t-mu_i)/s_i) *
    integral_l^u phi(z) exp(ik h_B'q_i z) dz.
Last factor is analytic complex Gaussian interval transform.
Reconstruct S_B by reverse integration of sum_i g_i, multiply other
blocks' fields via prefix/suffix products, then integrate t and k.
When all h_B=0 skip frequency axis exactly. No local quadrature.

New geometry: lift row i to P_i=(R_i[0],R_i[1],mu_i). Minimizing
performance is a linear program minimizing (z[0],z[1],1)' P_i over
conv{P_i}. Nonvertices have zero probability of unique minimum.
A vertex is a minimizer iff its incident edge directions have
nonnegative objective increment: tangent cone generated by those edges,
hence omitted rival inequalities redundant. Triangulated full hull is
safe superset of edge graph. Equal means use 2D hull instead. The
3D graph has O(b) edges (planarity), so total envelope storage and knot
count O(b) vs O(b^2) constraints unpruned; envelope preparation after
hull O(b log b), not a worst-case Quickhull guarantee. Qhull Qc,
fallback all pairs on failure or coplanar points, no joggling.
Floating-point hull pruning not certified; prune=False retains all
competitors. Hull vertices that only win for directions with last
coordinate negative have empty interval for all t. Total arithmetic
O(n L Q) at actual performance panels L and frequencies Q, plus
envelope lookups/hull prep. L may grow with n. Memory O(n L batch)
plus envelopes, full n outputs including zeros. A draft eagerly
constructed all-pair arrays even in hull route; corrected before commit.

No permanent ties in restricted grammar: nonproportional local rows
and positive marginal variances required; angles within roundoff
of proportional refused. With positive observation noise all distinct
base contrasts retain positive variance; at zero noise require three
active independent blocks, so no two-runner contrast can be proportional
to observation. No ridge; roundoff-only rescaling if observation
variance just above one. Arbitrary PSD outside grammar, proportional
rows (even unequal means), zero marginal variances and positive-
probability ties unsupported, not silently mapped to different target.

Convergence: for finite t window and k cutoff boundary functions
continuous (empty interval zero), so composite Simpson and
Gauss-Legendre refinement converges. For each untouched active
block, condition on residual orthogonal to h_B: survival restricts
scalar Gaussian score to an interval. Integration by parts gives
|S_B|<=min(1,sqrt(2/pi)/(|k| ||h_B||)). Two untouched active blocks
at zero observation noise give integrable O(k^-2) tails or faster
after integrating |g_i|<=f_i. Positive observation noise gives Gaussian
decay. Thus K->infinity, pad->infinity with K Phi(-pad)->0 and
quadrature refinement on each finite rectangle gives exact original
target in real arithmetic. Performance truncation and block survival
reconstruction error bounded as iteration 11; no sampling or
covariance-fit error. Floating-point hull decisions, complex
cancellation and finite quadrature distinct numerical errors. No
a posteriori certified accuracy claimed. Tiny negative numerical
zeros (e.g. -7e-48) not clipped.

Prior art checked: Barber, Dobkin, Huhdanpaa (1996), The Quickhull
Algorithm for Convex Hulls, DOI 10.1145/235815.235821 (Princeton
TR-565-95 abstract); Dutt (1973), DOI 10.1093/biomet/60.3.637;
Genz (1992), DOI 10.1080/10618600.1992.10477010 (publisher abstracts).
Hull/normal-cone geometry and Fourier conditioning are established;
contribution is sparse geometry in all-winner complex survival
cavity. No novelty priority claim.
